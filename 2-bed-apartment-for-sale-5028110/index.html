The Property Shop are delighted to present this excellent two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment, ideally situated in the highly sought-after Summerseat development in Clonee. Extending to an impressive 81.96 sq. m. (approx.), this spacious property offers generous living accommodation, excellent natural light and a peaceful setting overlooking a beautifully maintained green area to the rear.

A particular highlight is the recently paved private patio to the front, offering a pleasant outdoor space and an attractive outlook. Double doors provide direct access to the patio, creating a seamless connection between the living accommodation and the outdoors.

Internally, the apartment comprises a spacious open-plan living and dining area, a well-appointed kitchen, two bedrooms and two bathrooms. The accommodation is bright, well proportioned and ideally suited to modern living.

Presented in excellent condition throughout, the property further benefits from an excellent B2 energy rating, gas-fired central heating and ample communal parking.

Located in the popular Summerseat development, the property is within a short stroll of Clonee Village and all its amenities, making this an ideal home for first-time buyers, downsizers or investors.
